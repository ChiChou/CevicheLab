### Chromatic — Thomas Eboli; Chang, Kang and Kang

[Publisher page](https://www.ipol.im/pub/art/2023/443/), [source archive](https://www.ipol.im/pub/art/2023/443/443-main.zip).
- Upstream source: MIT, **Copyright (c) 2022 Thomas Eboli**. The complete license is retained in [tools/chromatic/LICENSE.upstream](tools/chromatic/LICENSE.upstream) and in the executable's resource [license](tools/chromatic/Sources/ChromaticCore/LICENSE.upstream). The paper's publication terms are separate from the source-code MIT grant.
- Included scope: [CPUReference.swift](tools/chromatic/Sources/ChromaticCore/CPUReference.swift), [Chromatic.metal](tools/chromatic/Sources/ChromaticCore/Chromatic.metal), and the reference-output fixtures in [ReferenceFixtureTests.swift](tools/chromatic/Tests/ChromaticCoreTests/ReferenceFixtureTests.swift). The package's image I/O, parameter validation and command-line integration are project code.
- Changes: Swift CPU and Metal implementations, explicit buffer initialization, boundary handling and corrections to the reference implementation; the [package README](tools/chromatic/README.md) enumerates the behavioral differences. These are modified implementations, not unmodified upstream code.
- Audit: the complete MIT license in the publisher's current archive matches the notice retained here. Archive SHA-256 on the audit date: `145738382c72d54e4c0332890cd102d54e452cccccc8426cc7ea11cd28520f0d`. This identifies the archive checked now, not a claim that the original port recorded that archive hash.

## Algorithm and workflow references

- **Benedikt Bitterli**, [*Star Stacker: Astrophotography with C++11*](https://benedikt-bitterli.me/astro/): star localization, coarse/fine alignment, shared camera geometry and reprojection/averaging. Our [joint mean implementation](tools/starstack/TENERIFE-JOINT-MEAN.md) is independently written and differs in detection, distortion modeling, masking and background policy. No upstream code, images or explicit code-reuse license was established; citation does not authorize copying them.
- **HGMeteorTool / HoshinoPanoAssistant**, [HG731GZ/HGMeteorTool](https://github.com/HG731GZ/HGMeteorTool): camera/sky-coordinate workflows, timing, PSF fitting and residual calibration ideas. The [review](tools/starstack/HGMETEORTOOL-REVIEW.md) records inspected blobs. No standalone source license was established; no code or models were imported.
- **Siril**, [registration](https://siril.readthedocs.io/en/stable/preprocessing/registration.html) and [stacking](https://siril.readthedocs.io/en/stable/preprocessing/stacking.html): practical registration, rejection and trail-stack references. Our implementation does not copy Siril source.
- **OpenCV**, [geometric transforms](https://docs.opencv.org/4.x/da/d54/group__imgproc__transform.html): the CPU mean uses `remap`; the Metal sampler uses a coefficient table generated by calling the installed OpenCV implementation to match its Lanczos4 sampling phases. No OpenCV source or binary is vendored.
- **Adobe DNG**, [resources/specification](https://www.adobe.com/support/downloads/dng/dng_sdk.html), **Apple** [ImageIO](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/imageio), [Core Image](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/coreimage), [Core ML](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/coreml), [Metal](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/metal), [AVFoundation](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/avfoundation), and **ExifTool** [EXIF tags](https://github.com/exiftool/exiftool/blob/master/html/TagNames/EXIF.html): format/API documentation. Platform frameworks are used under their own terms; no Apple or Adobe SDK source is bundled.

Additional repositories examined but not incorporated are identified in the [repository survey](tools/starstack/REPOSITORY-SURVEY.md), including Astraios, strehl, photonloom, GPUStacker and AstroBurst. The [model/paper ledger](docs/research/photography-workflows-2026-10/models-and-licenses.md) covers XFeat/LighterGlue, LightGlue and its features, PixelStitch, UniStitch, RoMa, UDIS++, RopStitch, Depth Anything, SAM, PNNP, DEBIR, ASTERIS, GraXpert and focus-stacking research. [Earlier model research](experiments/linear-dng/DENOISE-MODELS.md) covers additional denoisers. These are references or proposals unless an experiment explicitly says otherwise; their weights are not part of this repository. The ledger's review dates and unresolved terms remain authoritative for what was actually checked.
